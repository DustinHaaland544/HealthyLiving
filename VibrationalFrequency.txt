Vibrational Frequency

Vibrational Frequency refers to the rate at which atoms, molecules, or objects oscillate and resonate. The term appears across both scientific physics and 
spiritual or alternative wellness traditions.

Scientific and Medical Definitions

i) Physics: In physics, everything is made of atoms that constantly move and oscillate. Brainwaves are also measured in specific frequencies using hertz (Hz):

    - Delta Waves: 0.5 to 4 Hz (deep sleep)

    - Theta Waves: 4 to 8 Hz (meditation and REM sleep)

    - Alpha Waves: 8 to 14 Hz (calm and relaxed states)

    - Beta Waves: 14 to 30 Hz (active thinking and focus)

ii) Whole Body Vibration (WBV): A physical therapy and exercise method that uses mechanical vibration platforms to help with muscle recovery, mobility, 
    and conditions like Parkinson's disease or osteoporosis. It is unsafe for people with pacemakers, blood clots, or pregnancy.

iii) Alternative Claims: Unverified holistic theories claim that the human body resonates around specific megahertz (MHz) levels and that low frequencies cause 
     illness, though mainstream science notes these specific body-frequency claims lack empirical proof.

Spiritual and Wellness Concepts

- Emotional Scale: In New Age and spiritual communities, "raising your vibration" is used as a metaphor for shifting from negative emotions (like fear or 
  anger) to positive emotions (like love or gratitude).

- Practices to Raise Vibration: Proponents suggest daily habits like meditation, positive affirmations, spending time in nature, deep breathing, and eating 
  whole or plant-based foods.

- Community Perspectives: Discussions on forums like Reddit show mixed opinions; some users view high frequencies as a useful mindset framework for personal 
  growth, while others note it is purely a symbolic concept rather than a literal scientific measurement.
